## Platform

web

## What it is

Personal portfolio of Zdenek Ferenc, a developer and founder in Brno. Czech-language site. It presents his own startup (RiseHigh), client projects (Alexander Kovačka, Hravé slovíčka), services with a free design and performance audit, a DevLog, and contact (Cal.com call, e-mail, contact modals).

## Users and jobs

- **Primary: potential clients** looking for someone who can design and build a whole web product end to end. They should leave convinced he thinks about the product, not just the code, and get in touch.
- **Secondary: people curious about the startup** (companies, peers, school, recruiters). They want the founder story, traction and reasoning.

## Positioning

One person who handles everything from UI to backend, mostly on Next.js and Supabase, and builds things from zero that are fast and make sense for the people who use them. Case studies are written as honest first-person stories, including what did not work.

## Product facts to preserve

- RiseHigh: platform connecting students with companies through real challenges (risehigh.io). Started inside the VUT ESBD programme. Story beats: Virtigo Digital agency, VUT Hub concept, EuroWeek in Finland with TIMI Academy, Figma prototype tested with HR and managers, one-person summer build (Next.js, Supabase, Tailwind), 24h challenge at INPROF for Sportrera, first open challenge with 32 sign-ups from VUT, MUNI and VŠB.
- Only one RiseHigh screenshot exists (`public/risehigh.webp`). Anything else shown must be labelled as illustrative.
- No invented numbers, clients, testimonials or claims.

## Voice

Czech, informal "ty", short plain sentences, first person, honest about failures. No marketing hype.
